## Later routes that raised an exception in a mutant (downstream effects)

- governance:issuerPopBeforeAccreditation: 10 later routes (KL4, PV1, PV12, PV5, RL-a, RL-b, RL-c, RL-d2, RL-e, RL-f) raised an exception instead of reaching their catalogued check.
- governance:issuerPopBeforeAccreditation: with the controller PoP check removed, governance
  accredits the squatter's controller for issuer A (SQ-a and SQ-b are accepted). The honest
  wallet then takes A's controller from that accreditation and refuses A's holders at
  issuerPop (fail closed), so later routes that use A's holders stop with that refusal.

## Classes

- redundant: another named defence stops every route that the removal would open; kept as
  defence in depth, and the backing defence is listed.
- robustness: a check that turns a failure into a clean refusal (no security role); its
  removal leaves a later check or the circuit to fail.
- untested: no route exercised it; a route was added and the row re-run.
